**Problem:** Develop a novel method for constructing n-point configurations in the unit square [0,1]² that maximize the minimum-area triangle formed by any 3 of the points — Heilbronn's triangle problem. Current state-of-the-art for small n is well-tabulated (Cantrell 2003, Goldberg, Comellas-Yebra 2002; recent improvements 2020-2023): n=10 gives min-area ≈ 0.0376, n=15 ≈ 0.0322, n=20 ≈ 0.01794, n=25 ≈ 0.01163, n=30 ≈ 0.00832. These are found by simulated annealing, basin hopping, or other numerical optimization on the discontinuous min-area objective. The asymptotic bound is c log n / n² ≤ H(n) ≤ O*(2^(O(√log n))/n^(8/7)) (Cohen-Pohoata-Zakharov 2023). The bottleneck for small n: standard local optimizers get stuck in local minima because the min-triangle objective is non-smooth (only the smallest triangle's gradient counts), so most degrees of freedom feel no force. Find compositions that either (a) bridge Heilbronn-style point-placement optimization with concepts from adjacent fields where the connection has not been explicitly developed — e.g. determinantal point processes (repulsive sampling with explicit density), Coulomb / log-gas potentials from statistical mechanics (where equilibrium measures give very well-spread points), Riesz s-energy minimization (Fekete points), low-discrepancy sequences (van der Corput, Halton, Sobol, lattice rules), orthogonal polynomial roots (Gauss-Legendre quadrature nodes), Lloyd's centroidal Voronoi tessellation, lattice constructions from coding theory and sphere packing, circle packing rigidity / Tutte embeddings, neural architecture search applied to point placements, graph neural networks for combinatorial geometric optimization, differentiable simulation with smoothed min approximations (logsumexp), or random matrix theory (eigenvalue repulsion in GUE/CUE) — or (b) compose two underconnected numerical-optimization or point-set papers that together suggest a new search method. Goal: surface candidate methods concrete enough to implement in pure Python (numpy/scipy/jax) and benchmark min triangle area achieved on n=10, 15, 20, 25, 30 against the tabulated published baselines. A method either beats the published value at some n (signal) or doesn't (calibration).
