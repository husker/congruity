**Problem:** Develop a novel quantization scheme for dense sentence/text embeddings (typically 384–1024 dimensions, float32) that achieves higher recall@10 at fixed bits-per-vector than current state-of-the-art (Product Quantization PQ, Optimized Product Quantization OPQ, RaBitQ 2024, Matryoshka representation learning, simple scalar quantization to int8 / int4) on standard sentence-embedding benchmarks (MTEB, BEIR, MS MARCO).
