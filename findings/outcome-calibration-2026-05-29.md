## Verdict

1. **The L1–L4 cascade has no discriminative power on what gets verified.** Every joined composition shares the *same* signature: L3=weak, purely_testable=True, all_passed=False, objections_fundamental=0, levels_passed=2. The cascade cannot predict outcome because it is constant across the verified set.
2. **LLM `confidence` is the only signal that tracks outcome** (≥8 → ~57% positive vs <8 → ~20%), yet it is not the primary ranking key.
3. **L3 never says "sound" and `all_passed` is always False** — the sound/weak distinction and the all_passed gate are dead weight as currently calibrated (consistent with the known L3 over-firing risk).
4. **The gate over-fires on BOTH axes — confirmed by the gate-precision series (3 rejected compositions verified, 2 over-fires).** C5 cache policy (L3=invalid) → clean-negative (correct). C3 micro-LM (L3=invalid) → partial-positive (L3 over-fire). C2 BPE coder (L3=invalid AND purely_testable=False) → **clean-positive, the best result in the project** (over-fire on both axes). L4's `purely_testable` is NOT a safe replacement gate: it fired on a pigeonhole objection that is true of every compressor and irrelevant to the metric. Neither L3's verdict nor `purely_testable` is a trustworthy hard filter.

## Recommended actions

- **Stop using L3 verdict and `purely_testable` as hard filters.** The gate-precision series shows both over-fire (2 of 3 rejected compositions were winners, incl. the project's best). With verification ~1 session each, PRIORITIZE candidates and verify top-k regardless of L3/L4 verdict.
- **Fix L4's fundamental/empirical split** to ask whether an objection bears on the practical metric (precludes beating baselines on realistic data), not whether the *literal/universal* claim is false. A pigeonhole-type objection that applies equally to all baselines must be classified non-disqualifying.
- **Promote `confidence` into the ranking** (or blend it with objections_empirical), since it is the only outcome-predictive signal observed.
- **Recalibrate the L3 prompt** so "sound" is achievable, or collapse the verdict to the binary it actually is and stop reporting `all_passed`.
- **Backfill C-ids** for the two pre-convention findings so future joins are 100%.
